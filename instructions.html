EXAMINATION INSTRUCTIONS

Candidates are required to observe the following rules:

  • The examination duration is 50 minutes. Candidates must manage their time effectively.
  • Candidates must complete the examination independently without assistance from another person.
  • Impersonation, communication with others, collusion and all forms of examination malpractice are strictly prohibited.
  • Candidates must carefully read each question and select the most appropriate answer.
  • Candidates are advised to review their answers before submitting, where time permits.
  • Candidates must remain within the examination environment throughout the examination.
  • The use of unauthorised assistance or materials is prohibited.
  • Candidates are expected to maintain a stable internet connection throughout the examination.
  • Camera and microphone monitoring will be used for examination supervision and integrity.
  • Candidates must remain visible and maintain an appropriate examination environment.
  • Any suspicious activity or violation of examination rules may be reported to the examination administrators for review.
  • Candidates should not refresh or close the examination page unnecessarily.
  • Temporary internet interruptions do not extend the official examination time.
  • The examination will be automatically submitted when the official time expires.
  • Once submitted, an examination cannot be restarted by the candidate.
